BUZZNET · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information BuzzNet may collect, how it may be used, and the choices users may have over their information.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Information We May Collect

When you use BuzzNet, information may be provided directly by you or generated through your use of the platform.

  • Account information such as your name, username and email address.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Content you create, upload or share.
  • Messages and other communications you send through BuzzNet.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate and improve BuzzNet and provide its features.

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide social and communication features.
  • Personalize parts of your BuzzNet experience.
  • Maintain platform security.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, spam or other prohibited activity.
  • Improve platform performance and features.

3. Information You Share

BuzzNet is a social platform. Content you intentionally make public may be visible to other users.

Avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly. Once information is shared online, other people may copy or redistribute it.

4. Messages

BuzzNet may provide private messaging features. Information associated with those communications may be processed as necessary to provide, secure and maintain the messaging service.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

BuzzNet may use technologies such as cookies, local storage, or similar mechanisms to remember preferences, maintain sessions and support platform functionality.

The current development version of BuzzNet may store certain demo information locally on your device.

6. AI and ik

BuzzNet may provide AI-powered features through its assistant known as ik.

When AI functionality is connected to a third-party AI service, information submitted to that service may be processed according to the applicable service's policies and terms.

Do not submit passwords, financial credentials, private keys, or other highly sensitive information to an AI assistant.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with BuzzNet.

However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

BuzzNet may eventually integrate third-party services for features such as authentication, hosting, analytics, AI, payments, or other functionality.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

BuzzNet should only be used by people who are legally permitted to use the service in their jurisdiction.

If the platform is intended for a particular minimum age, that requirement will be stated in the applicable service rules.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the features available, users may be able to review, update, or remove certain account information through their BuzzNet settings.

Additional privacy controls may be added as the platform develops.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or fulfill other legitimate purposes.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as BuzzNet develops. The updated version will include a revised date when appropriate.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official BuzzNet contact method provided by the platform.
