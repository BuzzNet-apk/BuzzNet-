OUR PURPOSE

Built for meaningful connection

BuzzNet aims to create a social experience that feels simple, expressive, intelligent, and enjoyable.

Social media should be more than endless scrolling. It should give people a place to share ideas, discover interesting content, communicate with others, and build communities.

BuzzNet is being developed around that idea. The platform brings together a personalized feed, exploration, notifications, messaging, profiles, settings, and AI assistance.

Our goal is to keep improving the experience while putting usability, creativity, security, and responsible technology at the center.
