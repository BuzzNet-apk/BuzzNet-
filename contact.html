Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a BuzzNet account?

Open the BuzzNet signup page, enter your account information, accept the applicable terms, and continue through the registration process.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page. A real password-recovery system will be connected when BuzzNet's backend is added.

How can I report inappropriate content?

Use the appropriate reporting feature when available, or contact BuzzNet support with enough information for the issue to be investigated.

What is ik?

ik is BuzzNet's planned AI assistant. It is being designed as an integrated AI experience inside BuzzNet.
